Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

This policy explains what personal data ParTrack collects, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have. ParTrack is a golf round-tracking app for everyday players.

1. Who we are

ParTrack (“we”, “us”) is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at partrack.app@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account: your email address, and a password which is stored securely (hashed) by our authentication provider — we never see it in plain text.
Profile: your display name, handicap, preferred tees, distance units and notification preferences.
Golf data: the rounds you record (course, tees, format and dates), your hole-by-hole scores and penalties, any optional shot details (club and lie) you log, and the names of other players or guests you add to a round.
Community data: communities you create or join, your membership, and any rounds or messages you choose to share to them.
On-device data: an in-progress round is also stored locally on your device so scoring works offline.

3. What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your location or GPS data. We do not use advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your data.

4. How we use your data

To provide the app: saving your rounds, calculating scores and stats, and syncing across your devices. To authenticate you and keep you signed in. To show rounds you choose to share with communities you belong to. To send the app notifications you have opted into (round reminders, community activity, tips) — controlled by your settings.

5. Legal basis (UK GDPR)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the app you signed up for), for our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, and on the basis of your consent for optional notifications, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

6. Who we share it with

Other users: when you share a round to a community, members of that community can see it, including the player names on that round. Service providers: we use Supabase to host our database and handle authentication; Supabase processes this data on our behalf under our instructions. We may disclose data if required by law. We do not share your data with advertisers.

7. Storage and security

Your data is stored with Supabase and protected by row-level security, so only you — and communities you share to — can access your rounds. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Where data is processed outside the UK, it is protected by appropriate safeguards.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account, which removes your authentication record and purges your associated rows (profile, rounds, scores, shots and the communities you created). Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can update your profile and delete your account in-app, or contact us at partrack.app@gmail.com for anything else. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children

ParTrack is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: partrack.app@gmail.com.
